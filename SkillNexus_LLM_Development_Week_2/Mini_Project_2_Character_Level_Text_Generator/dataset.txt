Mira lived in a small house beside a quiet garden. Every evening, she watched the moon rise above the trees. One night, a silver path appeared between the flowers. Mira followed the path and found a tiny gate. Behind the gate, a little fox guarded a basket of glowing seeds. The fox told Mira that every seed could grow into a star flower. Mira planted one seed near the old stone wall. By morning, a bright flower had opened, and its petals shone like the moon. Mira returned to the garden each night and planted another seed. Soon the garden was filled with gentle light. The little fox visited often, and together they watched the stars above the trees. Mira learned that small acts of care could make an ordinary place feel magical.
