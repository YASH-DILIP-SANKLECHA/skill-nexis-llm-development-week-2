The little bird sat beside the window.
Every morning the sun warmed the quiet garden.
A gentle wind moved through the green trees.
The bird watched children walking along the road.
Some children carried books and some carried flowers.
At noon the garden became bright and full of sound.
The bird sang a short song from the old wooden fence.
A cat rested under a tree and listened to the song.
When evening came the sky turned orange and gold.
The children returned home while the bird stayed in the garden.
Night arrived slowly and stars appeared above the houses.
The next morning the sun rose and the bird began to sing again.
A small stream crossed the garden and flowed between smooth stones.
Rain fell softly and made the leaves shine.
After the rain the air smelled fresh and the clouds moved away.
The bird found seeds near the fence and shared the space with sparrows.
The cat stretched, yawned, and walked toward the warm doorstep.
The garden changed with every season but remained a peaceful place.
In spring new flowers opened beside the path.
In summer the trees gave shade to anyone who rested beneath them.
In autumn dry leaves covered the ground like a brown blanket.
In winter the cold wind passed over the quiet garden.
Each day brought a small change and each change became part of the story.
